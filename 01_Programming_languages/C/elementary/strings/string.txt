STRING PALINDROME

STRING = R A C E C A R
Index  = 0 1 2 3 4 5 6


Clearly ,
[0] = [6] (R)
[1] = [5] (A)
[2] = [4] (C)

now , n = 7 , flag = 0
      i = 0
      j = n-1 = 6 

i!=j | s1[i]!=s1[j] | flag   | i++ | j-- |
0!=6 | s1[0]!=s1[6] |   0    |  1  | 5   |
1!=5 | s1[1]!=s1[5] |   0    |  2  | 4   |
2!=4 | s1[2]!=s1[4] |   0    |  3  | 3   |
3!=3 | --> loop terminates

flag == 0 -> palindrome

eg 2.)

STRING = H S U S E
Index  = 0 1 2 3 4 

now , n = 5 , flag = 0
      i = 0
      j = n-1 = 4 

i!=j | s1[i]!=s1[j] | flag   | i++ | j-- |
0!=4 | s1[0]!=s1[4] |   1    |  1  | 3   |
1!=3 | s1[1]!=s1[3] |   1    |  2  | 2   |
2!=2 | --> loop terminates

flag = 1 -> Not palindrome.
